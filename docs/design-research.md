# Tretmax Games art direction

Research reviewed 27 October 2026. Public websites were reviewed for design and documented local event details; no page layout, logo, photography, player identity, or game publisher asset was copied.

## Creative direction

**Observed in the international references:** FACEIT leads with competitive play and routes visitors toward game-specific competition. ESL puts named events, dates, venues, and direct event actions beside large campaign imagery. start.gg makes event discovery and ranking easy to find in the core navigation. BLAST keeps event schedules, team/player information, and editorial coverage together within a game hub.

**Applied principles:** make the first tournament action obvious; keep status, date, game, entry, prize and format legible; move between expansive campaign framing and compact factual event information; retain a short, task-oriented mobile menu.

**Original Tretmax interpretation:** an editorial event-poster system with a warm paper surface for discovery, graphite event panels, acid-lime accents, condensed display type and a bespoke angular TM badge. The new homepage uses an original illustrated competition stage and a prominent sample event panel; lower sections vary between a paired event board, a three-panel game roster and a compact participation guide. Motion is limited to a single entrance for the hero image and copy plus image-scale hover feedback. Reduced-motion settings disable animation. No glow-heavy sci-fi motif, fake live feed, fabricated player, invented venue, or competitor branding is used.

## Ugandan references and limits

- **Game City Uganda:** its public site describes console, racing, VR, and tournament services at Uganda locations. Its current FIFA 26 listing identifies a Kampala HQ venue, a UGX 15,000 entry, group stage plus knockouts, and projector displays. This supports making event logistics visible; it does not establish that all Ugandan esports is LAN-based. [Game City Uganda](https://gamecityug.com/)
- **thinkIT FMU Esports Championship:** the organizer page describes an FMU-sanctioned WRC rally-simulation series with Motorsport Africa Club, lists The FunStation Hub in Naalya as a venue, and publishes event dates, scoring, and standings. This demonstrates structured local competition and published results in a different game category. [thinkIT championship](https://thinkit.africa/EsportsChampionship)
- **Carry1st Africa Cup 2025 Uganda recap:** the publisher documents five CODM teams, a round-robin group stage, best-of-five matches, and playoffs in the Uganda qualifier. It is useful evidence for local mobile-team competition, but is a 2025 retrospective, not a current event listing. [Carry1st recap](https://carry1st.com/blog/carry1st-africa-cup-2025-uganda-qualifiers-recap)
- No local gaming photos or event posters are used. Their permissions, participant releases, and Tretmax affiliation are not established. The hero stage is a fictional, original vector illustration and is explicitly labelled as illustration.
- Existing tournament fixtures are limited to COD Mobile, EA Sports FC 25, and PUBG Mobile. The supported browse catalog also includes Free Fire, eFootball, 8 Ball Pool, and Chess as requested for the product; these four titles have no tournament fixtures in the current sample data. This does not claim that any of these games are Uganda's most popular. Local player language preferences, team imagery rights, and audience research remain unverified and should be gathered with community consent before adding stories or documentary visuals.
